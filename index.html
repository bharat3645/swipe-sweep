Swipe & Sweep: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026 · Developer: Bharat Singh Parihar

Swipe & Sweep ("the app") helps you review the photos and videos stored on your Android device and delete the ones you no longer need. This policy explains what the app accesses and what it does with it. In short, your photos and videos never leave your device.

Information the app accesses

  • Photos and videos on your device. After you grant the "Photos and videos" permission, the app reads your media library (images, videos and basic details such as date, size and folder name) so it can show the items to you one at a time. On Android 14 and later you can choose to share only selected photos.

How that information is used

  • Media is displayed and processed only on your device, to let you keep or delete items.
  • Your keep/delete decisions, simple statistics (such as how much space you freed) and settings are saved in the app's private storage on your device. They are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, upload, transmit, share or sell any personal data or media.
  • It does not have internet access. The app does not request the Internet permission.
  • It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs that collect data, and requires no account.
  • Its private data is excluded from cloud backups.

Deleting media

Nothing is deleted until you confirm in Android's own system dialog. By default, items are moved to the system Trash, where they can be restored for 30 days. If you choose permanent deletion in Settings, deleted items cannot be recovered.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
